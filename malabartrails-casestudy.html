Project Overview

Designing a Premium Kerala Travel Experience

A visually immersive tourism website concept created to help a travel brand present Kerala destinations, experiences, packages and services in a premium digital environment.

About the Project

Malabar Trails is a travel and tourism website concept designed around the beauty, culture and experiences of Kerala.

The concept was created to demonstrate how a modern tourism business can move beyond a basic travel-agency website and create a more memorable digital experience for potential travellers.

The website brings destinations, curated experiences, tour packages, transportation, testimonials and enquiry options into one structured platform.

The overall design combines Kerala-inspired colours, editorial typography, large visuals and spacious layouts to create a calm, sophisticated and premium travel experience.
