Project Overview

Designing a Modern Travel Storytelling Experience

A carefully structured travel blog website focused on visual storytelling, destination discovery, readability and responsive browsing.

About the Project

Panvi Chronicles is a premium travel blogging platform created for a passionate travel creator who wanted a professional online presence for sharing adventures, destination guides and travel experiences.

The website was designed to create a clean reading experience while maintaining modern aesthetics, smooth navigation and mobile responsiveness.

The project combines travel storytelling, destination showcases, YouTube integration, SEO-friendly content structure and performance-focused web development.

The overall design approach focuses on making travel content easy to discover, enjoyable to read and visually engaging across different screen sizes.
